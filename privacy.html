Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 4, 2026 · Last Updated: June 4, 2026

Life. Covered. is an AI-powered family logistics coordination service. Mary is an AI assistant — not a human. We take the privacy of your family's information seriously.

1. Who We Are

Life. Covered. is operated by Hourglass Strategies LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), doing business as Life. Covered. Our service is accessible at lifecovered.app and via SMS/WhatsApp at (432) 220-3767.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when using our service:

  • Contact information: Your name and phone number
  • Family information: Names and ages of children and dependents you choose to add
  • Schedule information: Events, pickups, appointments, and other scheduling data you send us
  • Village members: Names and phone numbers of family members or caregivers you add to your account
  • Timezone: Your time zone preference for reminder scheduling
  • Message history: Text conversations with Mary to provide context for responses
  • Payment information: Billing details processed securely by Stripe (we do not store payment card data)

We do not collect photos, videos, biometric data, or health information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Life. Covered. service
  • Send automated reminders, morning briefings, and coordination messages
  • Coordinate schedules between family members and village contacts you have added
  • Process payments and manage your subscription
  • Respond to your messages and provide customer support
  • Send service-related notifications including trial expiry and account updates

4. AI Processing

Mary is powered by Claude, an AI assistant developed by Anthropic. Your messages are processed by Anthropic's API to generate responses. Anthropic's privacy policy governs their handling of data. We use minimal context — typically your last 5 messages and upcoming events — in each API call to minimize data exposure.

We do not use your personal information to train AI models.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Anthropic: To power AI responses (message content processed transiently)
  • Twilio: To deliver SMS and WhatsApp messages
  • Stripe: To process payments securely
  • Supabase: To store your account and scheduling data
  • Village members you add: We send coordination messages to phone numbers you provide

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

6. Children's Privacy

Life. Covered. is designed for use by adults (parents and caregivers). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Information about children (names, ages, school schedules) is provided by parents or guardians and is used solely to provide the coordination service. We do not share children's information with third parties except as necessary to deliver the service.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account information and message history for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time by texting STOP or contacting us at privacy@lifecovered.app. Upon cancellation, we retain data for 30 days before permanent deletion to allow for account recovery.

8. Security

We use industry-standard security practices including encrypted data storage, secure API connections, and access controls. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of SMS messages by replying STOP at any time
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@lifecovered.app or text STOP to (432) 220-3767.

10. SMS and WhatsApp Messaging

By using Life. Covered., you consent to receive SMS and/or WhatsApp messages from us including service messages, reminders, and account notifications. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or contact privacy@lifecovered.app.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by sending a message to your registered phone number. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
